One shortcut, start to finish

  1. A keypress

    Press Ctrl + Alt + Space in the field you are typing in. On Mac, use Control + Option + Space.

  2. As long as you talk

    Talk in Arabic, English or both. Nuvra transcribes while you speak, and Esc cancels.

  3. A moment

    Press Enter on Windows or Return on Mac to finish. Clear English is pasted at your cursor.

  4. When you are ready

    Read it and send it yourself. Nuvra never presses Enter in your app.

Nothing you say gets lost

The pill stays small while you talk. If a paste can’t land, your words wait on the clipboard and in History, one click from Copy Copy. Models shows when Whisper is Ready Ready, and Settings turns the window to Egyptian Arabic ENع.

The window holds only History, Models and Settings. There is nothing to write in it: speaking always starts from the shortcut, wherever you are.

Questions before you install

Does Nuvra press Enter or send anything for me?

No. It pastes the text where your cursor was and stops there. Sending stays your action in your app. During a recording, Enter only finishes the recording.

Do I need an NVIDIA graphics card?

No. Local Whisper runs on the processor on both platforms. Windows can also use an NVIDIA GPU; the macOS preview uses the CPU, without Metal acceleration. Local models need a one-time download and several gigabytes of free disk space.

Which apps can it paste into?

Use it in text fields in your editor, terminal, chat window or AI coding tool. On macOS, the target app must expose its focused field through Accessibility. If Nuvra cannot verify the field or paste into it, it keeps the text in History and tries to copy it to the clipboard.

Does my audio leave my computer?

Local Whisper processes audio on your computer. If you choose Gemini, Granola, Cohere or Wispr Flow, the selected provider processes your audio online. Structured prompts and translation fallback send text through your Codex or Claude Code account.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

The installer does not have a Windows publisher certificate yet, so SmartScreen may ask you to confirm. Updates are checked against the project’s own signing key before they install.

What does the macOS preview need?

macOS 15 or newer, on Apple Silicon or Intel. Allow Microphone and Accessibility access for recording, global shortcuts and paste, then restart Nuvra. The preview is not Apple-notarized. Follow the macOS installation notes if the first launch is blocked. Updates are manual: quit Nuvra and replace it with the new app.

Can I use the app in Arabic?

Yes. Settings, then Language, switches the window and the pill to Egyptian Arabic, right to left. Your prompts stay in English.
